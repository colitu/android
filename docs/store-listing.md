# Google Play listing draft

**Name:** Colitu Secure VPN

**Short description:** Fast, private VPN access with transparent device-based plans.

**Full description:** Colitu protects network traffic through an account-based VPN service. New accounts receive one 24-hour trial device without a card. The app shows eligible servers supplied by the Colitu API, retrieves device-bound VPN configuration, and lets users view subscription and device status. Payments open the hosted Platega checkout; Colitu does not collect card, SBP, or cryptocurrency credentials.

Required before submission: current phone and tablet screenshots, feature graphic, support and privacy URLs, Data safety answers verified against the final binary, content rating, target-country pricing, physical-device smoke evidence, and a signed AAB from the protected workflow.
